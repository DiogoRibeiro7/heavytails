    Authors and Contributors¶

    HeavyTails is developed and maintained by researchers and practitioners interested in heavy-tailed distributions and extreme value theory.

    Lead Developer¶

    Diogo F. Ribeiro¶

    Position: Assistant Professor Affiliation: ESMAD - Polytechnic Institute of Porto, Portugal Email: dfr@esmad.ipp.pt ORCID: 0009-0001-2022-7072 GitHub: @diogoribeiro7

    Research Interests:

    • Heavy-tailed distributions
    • Extreme value theory
    • Statistical machine learning
    • Bayesian statistics
    • Copula models

    Background: Diogo F. Ribeiro is an assistant professor specializing in statistics and probability theory with a focus on heavy-tailed phenomena. His research applies extreme value theory to finance, insurance, and risk management.

    Acknowledgments¶

    Inspiration and References¶

    HeavyTails draws inspiration from several excellent libraries and resources:

    Python Libraries:

    • SciPy - Reference implementations for validation
    • NumPy - Numerical computing patterns (not a dependency)
    • statsmodels - Statistical modeling approaches

    R Packages:

    • POT (Peaks Over Threshold) by Mathieu Ribatet
    • evir by Alexander McNeil and team
    • tea by Jan Beirlant and team

    Academic Works: The implementation is based on rigorous mathematical foundations from:

    1. Embrechts, P., Klüppelberg, C., & Mikosch, T. (1997). Modelling Extremal Events for Insurance and Finance. Springer.

    2. Resnick, S. I. (2007). Heavy-Tail Phenomena: Probabilistic and Statistical Modeling. Springer.

    3. de Haan, L., & Ferreira, A. (2006). Extreme Value Theory: An Introduction. Springer.

    4. Hill, B. M. (1975). "A Simple General Approach to Inference About the Tail of a Distribution." Annals of Statistics, 3(5), 1163-1174.

    5. Pickands, J. (1975). "Statistical Inference Using Extreme Order Statistics." Annals of Statistics, 3(1), 119-131.
